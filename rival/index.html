RIVAL

The arena where fans step out of the stands to compete, not spectate

Football Gamified data iOS, Android, Web

Overview

RIVAL is the fan-built alternative to apps like LiveScore. Most football apps hold live scores, history and club data behind betting odds and affiliate ads. RIVAL takes the same raw material and turns it into something you play.

Before kick-off, live, and after the final whistle, games challenge your knowledge of the match and let you score on it.

The Games

Relive it

Rewind and On This Day turn your club's history into a game: guess the scoreline, name the lineups, place the match moments. Rewind is aimed at your next opponent; On This Day is aimed at today's date.

Call it

Takes and Season Predictor let you back your judgement. Predict a scoreline, a scorer and three takes, or call how a whole season finishes. Harder calls pay more, weighted quietly against real odds.

Who It's For

The football nerd who knows their club better than anyone around them and wants that known, especially fans of EFL and lower-league clubs that the big apps treat as an afterthought.

  • ●Deep club-history archive for lower-league sides
  • ●Each game keeps its own score, with no ladder to climb
  • ●Prove what you know, beyond the WhatsApp group

Under the Hood

  • ●Expo (React Native) app across iOS, Android and web
  • ●Supabase for Postgres, auth, realtime and edge functions
  • ●Live and historic data from SportMonks
  • ●RevenueCat for subscriptions

Built in public, as fans, not as a faceless studio.
